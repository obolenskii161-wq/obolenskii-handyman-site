Frequently asked questions

Do you provide engineering or architectural design?

No. Services are limited to non-licensed estimating and fabrication support based on client-provided designs. Structural calculations, code compliance determinations and engineered or stamped drawings are excluded.

What do I need to provide?

Current drawings, specifications, scope boundaries and any manufacturer requirements. PDF drawings are preferred for initial review; other formats can be discussed.

Can you help with recurring bids?

Yes, recurring estimating support can be discussed after a pilot project.

Tell me about your project.

Start with a short description. Drawings can be shared by email after scope and confidentiality requirements are agreed.
